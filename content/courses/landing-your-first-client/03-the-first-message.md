## Build around one observation

A useful first message is short enough to read on a phone and specific enough that it could only have been sent to that business. Use four parts: context, observation, consequence, and question. Keep your portfolio link in the signature unless the recipient asks how you work.

Leave out your life story, a catalogue of tools, claims that you can “grow their business,” and false urgency. You have not earned enough context to diagnose the whole company.

## Example: local introduction

```text
Hi Mara — Luis from the Saturday market suggested I contact you. [shared context]

I noticed the catering page lists three packages, but the enquiry form does not ask
which package a customer wants. [specific observation]

That probably means you have to clarify it by message each time. [limited consequence]
Are you already planning to change the form? [easy question]

— Ana
```

The wording leaves room for Mara to say the current process works. It also shows Ana followed the customer path before writing.

## Example: no introduction

```text
Hello Devon,

I found North Street Cycles while looking for repair shops near Quezon Avenue.
[credible source]

On a phone, the service prices run beyond the right edge of the page, so the last
column is hidden. [observable issue]

Would a screenshot with the affected screen size be useful? [small offer]

Thanks,
Paolo
```

Paolo offers evidence, not an unsolicited redesign. If Devon replies, Paolo can send the screenshot and ask whether customers have reported the issue.

## Check before sending

Read the message once as the recipient. Then verify:

- The business name and person’s name are correct.
- The observation is visible and current.
- The consequence is modest, not presented as a fact you cannot know.
- The question takes little effort to answer.
- The message has no attachment or surprise calendar link.

Follow up once after several business days with a new piece of context, such as the promised screenshot. Repeating “Did you see this?” adds pressure but no reason to respond.
