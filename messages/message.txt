🏆 **THE PLAYBOOK PH: TEAM TASK RULES & GUIDELINES**
**Season Window:** September 1 to December 31, 2026
**Teams:** 3 teams, ~208 members each
**Tasks:** Freelancing Agency, Short Film/Commercial Video, Digital Products, Full Branding

**1. THE OBJECTIVE**
Each team competes across 4 fronts this season: a Freelancing Agency, Short Film/Commercial, Digital Products, and Full Branding, applying what they've learned to build real skills and income. One team wins by executing best across all 4 by season's end.

If something isn't covered in this document, it goes to Paks directly, not group chat debate.

**2. HOW EACH TASK IS SCORED**
Each team competes in all 4 tasks at once, each with its own Sub-Leader.

• 🚀 Freelancing Agency: highest verified earnings, 40% weight
• 🎬 Short Film/Commercial: most total views across FB, YT, TikTok, IG combined, 25% weight
• 💻 Digital Products: most verified sales, 25% weight
• 🎨 Full Branding: community-wide vote (open to non-team members too), 10% weight

**Important:** Final scores are calculated by percentile rank against the other 2 teams, not raw numbers. This stops one team winning Agency just from drafting a member with existing high-ticket clients, and keeps Film views from being decided by one lucky viral post.

Branding has no standalone prize. It feeds directly into the Agency's website and presentation, so a weak branding effort can quietly hurt your Agency score too.

**3. MONEY, PAYOUTS, AND FUND CUSTODY**
• Clients are international: payments land in USD/foreign currency, then convert to PHP.
• Team Leaders hold funds and release cuts within 3 business days via GCash/bank transfer, confirmed with members beforehand.
• Shared Team Fund covers recurring costs, allocated jointly by Team Leader and Sub-Leaders.
• $500 pool contribution comes from the Team Fund, held by Paks (not Team Leaders) for neutrality.
• Leftover Team Fund balance is reported to Paks before redraft, no auto-rollover without sign-off.
• Paks takes a separate documentation cut; member payouts go out first.
• Disputes escalate: Sub-Leader, then Team Leader, then Paks, whose call is final.

**4. ELIGIBILITY RULES**
• No outside help. No pre-existing clients, no non-member freelancers, no friends-and-family shortcuts. Everything built and sourced from scratch starting Day 1.
• Ineligible client = any relationship that existed, in any form, before the season start date (past clients, personal contacts you've been paid by before, leads already in conversation pre-launch).
• AI tools and workflows from inside The Playbook are encouraged. New tools released mid-season are fair game for everyone the moment they drop.
• Sub-Leaders are appointed by the Team Leader and must consent before it's official.
• Members self-select their task. You can only count toward one task's numbers per season.

**5. WHO QUALIFIES FOR A PAYOUT**
Being drafted onto a team doesn't automatically make you eligible for a cut. To get paid from a project:
1. Your Sub-Leader must vouch that you meaningfully contributed, and
2. You must meet a minimum activity bar in your task's assigned channel (not general chat).

Message count in general channels does not count.

**6. FAIR PLAY AND MID-SEASON CHECKS**
• Biweekly (2x a month) team meetings are mandatory progress checks, not optional catch-ups.
• Every biweekly meeting, Sub-Leaders get asked directly: "Has anyone tried to bend a rule this cycle?" This is how we catch problems while there's still time to fix them.
• Payment screenshots are required for every claimed earning. A live earnings tracker is optional but earns bonus credibility.
• Spot a loophole or grey area? Report it to your Sub-Leader or Paks directly. Reporting doesn't get you penalized, staying quiet about one you're exploiting does.

**7. IF A TEAM OR SUB-TEAM FALLS APART MID-SEASON**
• If a Sub-Leader steps down or goes inactive, the Team Leader appoints a replacement, priority to proven former Sub-Leaders.
• If a whole sub-task group goes quiet, the team doesn't automatically forfeit that category. Paks may reshuffle members from other sub-tasks to help, at his discretion.

**8. END OF SEASON**
• Overall team winner is announced based on final percentile-rank scores across all 4 categories.
• Prize pool: WINNER TAKES ALL. Each of the 3 teams contributes $500 to a shared pool before the season starts, total pool = $1,500. The winning team takes the full $1,500.
• New draft night follows shortly after: top 90 most active members get hand-picked by (newly voted) Team Leaders, remaining members are shuffled in by Paks.

Questions on anything in this document go to Paks directly. This document can be amended mid-season if a loophole or unfair advantage needs to be closed, with or without prior notice. Non-loophole changes wait until next season.
